h1. AI

Different AI types behave differently.

* Stationary
** A stationary gun platform.  Fire towards the player, always brake, never thrust.

* Fighter
** Aggressive.  Charge the player, stay moving.

* Warship
** Not as mobile as the fighters.  Opts to hang back and bombard.

* Civilian
** Runs away when attacked.

* Agressive Civilian
** Will stay and fight until it starts losing, then runs away.

* Escort
** stay near a friendly target and defend it.  Do not persue the player past a certain distance from the ship its escorting.
